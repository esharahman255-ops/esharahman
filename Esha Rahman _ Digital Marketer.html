About me

I started in digital marketing by helping a small local business get its first online orders. That experience taught me that good marketing is clear messaging, the right audience and careful measurement, not luck.

Since then I’ve worked with brands of different sizes on strategy, advertising and content. I like campaigns that are simple to explain and easy to track.

My mission: help honest businesses be found, trusted and chosen online.
